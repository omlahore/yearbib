Seven cards for your story

Your bib with the year's distance on it, the totals, hours by sport, your season's profile, a training log, your biggest days, and your longest route. Each one is sized for an Instagram or WhatsApp story.

Three steps to your recap

  1. Step 01

    Ask Strava for your data

    On strava.com open Settings, then My Account. Under Download or Delete Your Account, choose Get Started, then Request Your Archive.

  2. Step 02

    Download the zip

    Strava emails you a link, usually within a few hours. The zip holds everything you have recorded.

  3. Step 03

    Drop it on this page

    Pick the year and your colours, then save the cards or share them to your story.

Nothing leaves your device

Your zip is opened inside this browser tab. Yearbib has no accounts and no server that sees your activities. When you close the tab, the data is gone.

The code is open source if you want to check. Read it on GitHub.
