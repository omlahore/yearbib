Your year in sport, free.

Strava now charges $80 a year for its Year in Sport. Everything it needs is already in the data export Strava gives you for free. Drop that zip here and get your year as cards you can share.

Your file is read on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop your export zip here or choose the file. Big exports with photos are fine.

Getting your export

  1. On strava.com, open Settings, then My Account.
  2. Under "Download or Delete Your Account", choose Get Started, then Request Your Archive.
  3. Strava emails you a link to the zip, usually within a few hours. Download it and drop it above.
